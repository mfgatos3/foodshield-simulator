📚 Scientific Foundation & Biological Validation (Grade 10)

1. Newton's Law of Cooling/Heating

$$T(t) = T_{\text{amb}} + (T_0 - T_{\text{amb}}) \cdot e^{-kt}$$

Models convective heat transfer between ambient air in Ibague and poultry meat mass. Styrofoam (Styrofoam Box, $k = 0.008$) significantly delays thermal elevation compared to an uninsulated open crate ($k = 0.055$).

2. Dynamic Binary Fission Model

$$N(t) = N_0 \cdot 2^n \quad \text{where} \quad n = \sum \frac{\Delta t}{g(T)}$$

Doubling time $g(T)$ decreases rapidly with heat: below $5^\circ\text{C}$ bacteria remain dormant; but at temperatures between $20^\circ\text{C}$ and $35^\circ\text{C}$, cells divide every 10 to 15 minutes, triggering rapid exponential proliferation.

3. QMRA Beta-Poisson Dose-Response

$$D = N(t) \cdot m \cdot 0.01$$ $$P_{\text{inf}} = 1 - \left(1 + \frac{D}{51.45}\right)^{-0.1324}$$

FAO/WHO standard quantitative microbial risk assessment for Salmonella enterica. Ingested cross-contamination dose $D$ assumes a 1% transfer rate during handling, converting bacterial load into clinical infection probability.
